What is AutoMaskPro?

AutoMaskPro is a high-end FFGL plugin designed specifically for VJs and content creators seeking perfect chroma keying without the hassle. Compatible with Intel UHD graphics cards and optimized for maximum live performance fluidity.

Installation

Step 1: Copy the AutoMaskPro.dll file.
Step 2: Paste it into your Resolume plugins folder:
C:\Program Files\Resolume Avenue\plugins\vfx (or your custom path).
Step 3: Open Resolume. In the effects tab, search for "AutoMaskPro" and drag it onto your clip.

Parameters Guide

Auto_Pick
Activates automatic color sampling based on the corners of your video. Let the plugin decide for you!
Key_Color 1, 2, 3
Select up to 3 different tones to remove. Perfect for backgrounds with shadows or gradients.
Threshold (1-3)
Adjust the keying strength for each color. Use it to erase difficult areas.
Softness
Smooths the edges for perfect integration with the background.
Edge_Erode
"Shrinks" the alpha mask inwards. Use it to remove that hated "green halo" on the edges.
Spill_Removal
Removes green reflections leaking onto your object (especially on metallic logos).

⚡ Pro Tips

  • Black Protection: The plugin automatically protects dark areas inside your logo so they don't accidentally become transparent.
  • Preview Mode: Dial this slider up to see the checkerboard background or the Alpha channel directly. It helps a lot in cleaning up those last pixels!
